About Us

We are the heartbeat of the Kenyan event scene! As a team of event enthusiasts who pride themselves in precision and excellence, Event Zone was born from the discovery of a broken bridge between the organizer's vision and the revellers experience. Our goal is to fix that! Our seamless platform incorporates M-Pesa integration, data security, and a user-friendly interface. For the Kenyan reveller, no more 'systems down' or lost vouchers. For the organizer, a powerful tool to manage and promote their events.

Join us on this journey as we redefine the event experience in Kenya, one ticket at a time. From the busy streets of nairobi to the coastal vibes of Mombasa, we aren't just selling tickets; we're powering the moments that become memories.
